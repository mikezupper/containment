# Code of conduct

Containment welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, abilities,
and levels of experience. Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and other spaces representing this project.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, explain technical disagreements, and focus criticism on ideas and
code. Accept correction, respect people's time, and help others understand the
project's constraints. Do not pressure contributors to share personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks,
doxxing, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Respect requests to stop an
interaction or leave someone alone.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Mike Zupper using the **Get In Touch** link on
[mikezupper.com](https://mikezupper.com/). Include relevant links and explain what happened.
Share only the personal information needed to understand the report. For immediate
platform abuse, also use GitHub's reporting tools.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, give a warning, restrict an interaction,
or temporarily or permanently exclude a participant. The response should reflect
the severity and pattern of behavior, with an explanation where appropriate. Reports
will be handled discreetly; information may be shared when needed to investigate,
enforce the policy, or comply with legal obligations. Retaliation against someone who
reports a concern is itself a violation.

This policy applies to project spaces and to conduct when someone publicly represents
Containment. The same expectations apply to maintainers and contributors.
