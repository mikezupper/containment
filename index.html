A game of making space.

Inspired by the 1992 Windows game. The balls keep moving. You choose where to draw the line.

  1. 01 /

    Split the chamber

    Choose a vertical or horizontal wall. Click or tap an open square. Two wall ends grow in opposite directions.

  2. 02 /

    Keep the ends clear

    A ball hitting a growing end costs a life and breaks that half. A half that reaches a boundary stays solid.

  3. 03 /

    Claim 75%

    Enclosed spaces with no balls fill in. Clear at least 75% of the original board before time or lives run out. The next level adds a ball.

Controls, scoring, and a useful first move

Pick your controls

Mouse
Click to build. Right-click to rotate.
Touch
Tap to build. Use the wall button to rotate. Drag to scroll without building.
Keyboard
Tab to the chamber. Use arrows to aim, Shift + arrows to move four squares, Enter or Space to build, R to rotate, and P or Escape to pause.

Give yourself an opening

Start with a long cut when the balls are far away. Once one side is safe, work inward. Only one pair of wall ends can grow at a time.

Each cleared square earns 10 points × level. Finish a chamber for a time bonus; capturing more than 80% or 90% earns an extra bonus. Choose Slow pace before starting or while paused. Switching tabs pauses the game.

This version starts with a 2:30 clock and adds 30 seconds per level. Timing and scoring are modern tuning choices, rather than an exact recreation of the Windows executable.
